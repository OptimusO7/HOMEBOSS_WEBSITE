Our story

Born from
a simple vision.

From IT services to a single intelligent system for the home.

Optimus Technologies was founded with a vision to provide IT services and technology solutions. In a world where technology evolves rapidly, we saw an opportunity to create a unified, intelligent system that truly understands and adapts to homeowners' needs.

HomeBoss emerged from countless hours of research, development and a deep understanding of what modern homeowners want: simplicity, security and smart automation that just works. The prototype goes live on August 8, 2026, with the full product launching in 2030.
